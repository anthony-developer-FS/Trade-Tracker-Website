Features

Everything you need to understand your trading

  • Import in seconds

    Upload a file from your broker. We detect the format, map the columns and rebuild every trade from its fills. Fix a column once and we remember it.

  • Every market, its own view

    Stocks, options, futures and crypto each get their own tab and stats, with contract multipliers handled for you.

  • Lists for each account or strategy

    Name your own lists, like "Schwab options" or "Day trades", and filter every chart by them.

  • Analytics that explain

    Win rate, profit factor, expectancy and R-multiples by setup, tag, weekday and hour, plus an equity curve and P&L calendar.

  • Journal and tags

    Add notes, screenshots and tags like "Chased entry" or "Followed plan" to see which habits help and which hurt.

  • Private by default

    Your trades are visible only to you. We never ask for your broker password or store full account numbers.
