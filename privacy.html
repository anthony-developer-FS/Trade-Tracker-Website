The short version

  • Your trades, journal and screenshots are private to your account. We use them to run TradeLab for you and for nothing else.
  • We never ask for your broker username or password and never connect to your brokerage account.
  • We do not sell your personal information, share it for targeted advertising, or use it to train AI models.
  • We don't use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.
  • You can export or delete your data, and you have the rights described below wherever you live.

2. Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Account information. Your email address, a password (stored only as a secure hash by our authentication provider), and, if you choose, a display name, username, profile picture, time zone, base currency and display preferences. If you sign in with a third-party provider such as Google, we receive your name, email address and profile picture from that provider.
  • Trading records. The trades and fills you enter or import, such as the instrument (stock, option, futures contract or crypto pair), direction, quantity, prices, times, fees and commissions, and the P&L we calculate from them.
  • Broker account details. The broker you use, a nickname for each account, and optionally the last four characters of the account number so you can tell accounts apart. We never store full account numbers in your account records, and we never ask for your broker login.
  • Imported files. When you import trades, we store the original file you upload (for example a CSV export from Charles Schwab, thinkorswim, Fidelity, Robinhood, Interactive Brokers, Webull, Tradovate or NinjaTrader) and its rows, so we can re-process it if our importer improves and so you can undo an import. Broker exports can contain more than trades, such as an account number. Please remove anything you don't want stored before uploading.
  • Journal content. The lists, setups, tags, notes, ratings, mood scores, journal entries and chart screenshots you add.
  • Billing information. TradeLab is free during early access. When paid plans launch, payments will be handled by our payment processor, [PAYMENT PROCESSOR, e.g. Stripe]. We will receive your plan, billing status, billing country and postal code, and the card brand and last four digits, but never your full card number.
  • Messages to us. Your name, email address and anything you include when you contact us, report a bug, answer a survey or send us feedback.

Information collected automatically

  • Device and log data. Your IP address, browser and device type, operating system, pages and features used, the referring page, and the date and time of each request. Our hosting and database providers record this in server logs to keep the Service running and secure.
  • Approximate location. A country or region inferred from your IP address. We don't collect precise location.
  • Local storage. Small items stored in your browser to keep you signed in and remember your settings (see Cookies and local storage).

Sensitive information

Your trading history is financial information, and journal notes may reveal things about you. We treat all of it as confidential and use it only to provide the Service to you. We do not collect government ID numbers, full financial account numbers together with access credentials, precise location, biometric data or health data, and we ask that you don't put such information into notes or uploads.

3. How we use information

  • To provide the Service: create and secure your account, import and rebuild your trades from their fills, calculate P&L and statistics, show charts, calendars and breakdowns, and store your journal and screenshots.
  • To apply your plan: enforce plan limits (for example, the number of trade lists you can create) and, once paid plans launch, process payments and manage subscriptions.
  • To support you: answer questions, fix problems with imports, and send service messages such as sign-in links, security alerts and notices about changes to our Terms or this policy.
  • To keep the Service safe: detect and prevent fraud, abuse, security incidents and violations of our Terms.
  • To improve the Service: understand which features are used and where things break, using aggregated or de-identified information wherever possible. We will not attempt to re-identify de-identified information.
  • To send product news, only if you opt in. Every marketing email has an unsubscribe link.
  • To comply with law: meet legal, tax and accounting obligations and respond to lawful requests.

We do not use your trading data or journal content for advertising, we do not sell it, and we do not use it to train artificial intelligence or machine learning models. TradeLab's analytics describe your own past trades. We do not make automated decisions about you that produce legal or similarly significant effects.

4. Legal bases for processing (EEA and UK)

If you are in the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom or Switzerland, we rely on these legal bases:

  • Performance of a contract (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR): to provide the Service you signed up for, including storing and analyzing your trades and processing payments.
  • Legitimate interests (Article 6(1)(f)): to secure the Service, prevent abuse, fix bugs and improve features, and answer messages sent to us. We weigh these interests against your rights, and you can object at any time.
  • Consent (Article 6(1)(a)): for optional marketing emails and any non-essential cookies we may introduce. You can withdraw consent at any time without affecting earlier processing.
  • Legal obligation (Article 6(1)(c)): to keep records required by tax and accounting law and to respond to lawful requests from authorities.

6. Cookies and local storage

We use only the cookies and browser storage needed to run the Service: to keep you signed in, protect your session and remember settings such as your theme. These are strictly necessary, so they don't require consent under EU and UK law.

We don't use advertising cookies, cross-site tracking pixels or third-party analytics that build profiles across websites. If we add analytics or other non-essential cookies later, we will update this policy and, where the law requires it, ask for your consent first.

Global Privacy Control. We honor the Global Privacy Control (GPC) browser signal as a request to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information. Because we don't sell or share personal information, no further action is needed on your part.

Do Not Track. There is no common standard for responding to "Do Not Track" signals. Since we don't track you across other websites, our practices are the same either way.
